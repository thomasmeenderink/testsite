ABOUT DUTCHJET

Private flying, fairly priced

DutchJet connects travellers with empty flights and tailored flights. No membership, no hidden costs, and one point of contact from request to landing.

OUR STORY

Why DutchJet?

Every day, private jets fly empty to their next assignment or back to their home base. That is a waste: of the seats, of the fuel, and of the chance to fly private for a price far lower than you would expect.

DutchJet puts those empty flights on display and makes them easy to reserve. If your route is not among them, we find an aircraft that does fit and send you a tailored proposal.

Our goal is simple: to make private flying clearer and more accessible, with clear prices and personal contact.
