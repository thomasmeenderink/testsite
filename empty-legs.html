What is an empty leg?

A private jet that has dropped off a client often has to fly home empty or on to its next assignment. We call that empty flight an empty leg.

01

The flight is already set

The route and time are set by the original assignment. That is why the price is much lower than for a tailored flight.

02

You reserve the aircraft

Choose a flight and leave your details. The price is for the entire aircraft, however many seats you use.

03

Confirmed by the operator

An empty leg is only final once it is confirmed. If the original assignment changes, the empty leg can change too.
