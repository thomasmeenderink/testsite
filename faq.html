Empty legs

What is an empty leg?

An empty leg is a private jet flight without passengers, for example after a client has been dropped off and the aircraft has to return to its home base. Because the flight is already scheduled, you can often book it for a fraction of the normal charter price.

Why are empty legs so affordable?

The flight is already paid for by the original assignment. For the operator, any income from the empty flight is a bonus, which is why the price is much lower than for a tailored flight.

Do I pay per seat or for the entire aircraft?

The price of an empty leg is for the entire aircraft. You can travel with your own party up to the maximum number of passengers listed for the flight.

Can an empty leg still change?

Yes. An empty leg depends on the original assignment. If that changes, the time or route of the empty leg can change too, or the flight can be cancelled. Your reservation is therefore only final once the operator confirms it.

Can I book an empty leg on a different date or to a different destination?

The route and time are largely fixed. Small adjustments are sometimes possible; mention your wishes with your reservation. If you want to decide entirely where and when you fly, choose a tailored flight.

Requests and booking

How do I request a tailored flight?

Enter your departure, destination, date and number of passengers in the form on the site. You then receive a proposal without obligation. The flight is only booked once you agree.

Is a request without obligation?

Yes. A request or reservation through the site is an enquiry. You are only committed once you have accepted the proposal or the confirmation.

How soon will I hear back?

We respond as quickly as possible, especially for empty legs departing at short notice. You receive a reply at the email address you provide.

What if I want to change or cancel my trip?

The terms for changes and cancellations differ per flight and operator. You will find them in the proposal or confirmation you receive from us. If in doubt, get in touch with us.

Travelling and on board

When do I need to be at the airport?

When flying private you usually arrive shortly before departure, often around 15 minutes. You receive the exact time and the departure terminal with your confirmation.

How much luggage can I bring?

That depends on the aircraft. Smaller jets have less luggage space than larger ones. Tell us in your request how much and what kind of luggage you are bringing, such as golf bags or skis, and we will take it into account.

Can my pet come along?

On many private flights pets are allowed in the cabin, depending on the aircraft, the operator and the rules of the destination country. Mention your pet in your request.

Which travel documents do I need?

You need the same documents as for a scheduled flight, such as a valid passport or ID card and possibly a visa. Check the rules of your destination before departure.
