What is an empty leg?

An empty leg is a private jet flight without passengers, for example after a client has been dropped off and the aircraft has to return to its home base. Because the flight is already scheduled, you can often book it for a fraction of the normal charter price.

Do I pay per seat or for the entire aircraft?

The price of an empty leg is for the entire aircraft. You can travel with your own party up to the maximum number of passengers listed for the flight.

Can an empty leg still change?

Yes. If the original assignment changes, the time or route of the empty leg can change too, or the flight can be cancelled. Your reservation is therefore only final once the operator confirms it.

Is a request without obligation?

Yes. A request or reservation through the site is an enquiry. You are only committed once you have accepted the proposal or the confirmation.

When do I need to be at the airport?

When flying private you usually arrive shortly before departure, often around 15 minutes. You receive the exact time and the departure terminal with your confirmation.

Choose the route you are interested in and leave your email address. As soon as there is an empty leg on that route, you are the first to know. Leave From or To empty for all routes.
