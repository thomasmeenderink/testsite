WHAT IS CHARTER

An entire aircraft, just for you

With a private jet charter you hire the complete aircraft including crew. You decide the departure time, the route and who flies with you. There are no stopovers you do not want and no fixed timetable.

That makes charter ideal for business trips with a tight schedule, trips with family or friends, or destinations that scheduled flights do not serve directly.

Prefer cheaper and flexible? Then check the empty legs first: flights that are already scheduled, at a fraction of the charter price.
